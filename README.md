I am an Associate Professor at UFOPA, Brazil, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data science, structural bioinformatics and molecular bioprospecting. My research investigates how molecular sequence, structure and physicochemical properties relate to the biological activity of peptides and natural products, with applications in drug discovery and biotechnology.

I coordinate PepSpace and lead an interdisciplinary research group focused on biomolecular technologies. My main interests include cell-penetrating peptides, blood–brain barrier-penetrating peptides, quorum-sensing peptides, chemical-space analysis and computational screening of bioactive compounds.

I hold a PhD in Biotechnology, with an emphasis on bioprospecting, and an MSc in Genetics and Molecular Biology, with an emphasis on bioinformatics, from the Federal University of Pará (UFPA). At UFOPA, I teach bioinformatics and structural biology and contribute to graduate training in Biosciences and the BIONORTE Biodiversity and Biotechnology
