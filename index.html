01 / About me

At the intersection of
molecules and computation.

I am Kauê Santana da Costa, an Associate Professor at the Federal University of Western Pará (UFOPA), Brazil. My work combines artificial intelligence, data science, structural bioinformatics and computational bioprospecting to investigate bioactive peptides and natural products.

I study how molecular sequence, structure and physicochemical properties relate to bioactivity, membrane penetration and molecular recognition. I coordinate PepSpace, an international research initiative and web server for exploring peptide chemical space.

I hold a PhD in Biotechnology, with an emphasis on bioprospecting, and an MSc in Genetics and Molecular Biology, with an emphasis on bioinformatics, from UFPA. At UFOPA, I teach bioinformatics and structural biology and contribute to graduate training through PPGBio and the BIONORTE network.

03 / Research platform

PepSpace.

A shared space for peptide sequence, structure and bioactivity.

As coordinator of PepSpace, I work with collaborators in Brazil, Belgium and Colombia to connect predictive models with accessible tools for peptide research.

The web server supports the investigation of CPPs, B3PPs and QSPs through three complementary modules.

Explore the web server ↗Platform documentation
01

Bioactivity prediction

BrainPepPass, ConvBoost-CPP and QuorumPep-Pred for peptide screening and prioritization.

02

Sequence analysis

Sequence comparisons, conserved amino acid patterns and local similarity to reference peptide classes.

03

Structure analysis

Structural and physicochemical characterization of peptides from PDB input.

The current web interface focuses on natural and synthetic non-modified peptides. Supported inputs depend on the selected module and model.
